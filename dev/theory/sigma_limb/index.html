The limb allowance σ_limb

The timing error σₜ of a contact describes only the measurement of the time. Real contacts also deviate from the fitted limb for other reasons:

  • topography: mountains and depressions of a few km, which an ellipse cannot follow;
  • shape: a limb that is not exactly an ellipse;
  • timing: errors not captured by σₜ, such as clock offsets, latencies, or a wrong exposure time.

σ_limb [km] is an allowance for all of these. It is added in quadrature to the uncertainty of every contact:

σ = √((σₜ |v|)² + σ_limb²)
  • Units: km at the body, i.e. along the chord for residual=:chord and radially for residual=:radial.
  • Scope: it applies to the contacts only. Negative chords keep their own tolerance (hard, or sensitivity × speed for :soft).
  • Values:
    • σ_limb = 0 (the default) uses the timing errors alone;
    • a number fixes it, e.g. from known topography (Kilic et al. (2026) use 4 km for Ixion);
    • fit_limb(...; σ_limb=:auto) finds the value for which χ²/dof = 1 (by bisection) and fits with it.

What it does to the fit.

  • Weights: they become more equal. Without it, a contact timed to 0.01 s dominates one timed to 1 s by a factor of 10⁴ in weight. With σ_limb = 5 km, neither can be trusted better than 5 km, which is what topography or a non-elliptical limb implies.
  • Errors: a, b, the position angle, the centre and everything derived from them get larger, consistent with the actual scatter of the contacts about the limb.
  • Scaling: scale_errors = true multiplies all uncertainties by √(χ²/dof) and keeps the relative weights. σ_limb instead adds the same amount everywhere, which suits scatter that does not depend on the timing precision.
  • Systematic offsets: a clock offset of one station is better modelled by its own parameter (fit_dt, with a prior). σ_limb is for random deviations.
  • SORA: the corresponding option of fit_ellipse is ellipse_error, fixed by the user.

How to read it. σ_limb from :auto estimates the rms deviation of the contacts from the fitted limb, beyond their timing errors. It is a property of the data together with the model:

Exampleσ_limbMeaning
Huya 20197–10 kmtiming problems of some stations, and possibly topography
1995 SM5513.5 kmdriven by two inconsistent Wise timings

With few degrees of freedom the estimate itself is uncertain: with one degree of freedom (2007 UK126) it means little.

In the plots.

  • The red contact segments show ±1σ of the timing only, i.e. σₜ |v|, without σ_limb, so that the timing precision of each station stays visible.
  • The bands (shape band, pointwise band, 68.3 % region) and the centre ellipse come from the fit's covariance or posterior, which include σ_limb. They are wider than with the timing errors alone.
  • A limb may therefore lie outside a station's red segment and still be consistent: the difference is absorbed by σ_limb.
  • When σlimb > 0, its value is printed with the fitted dimensions below the legend ("σlimb = … km").
